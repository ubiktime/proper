The Unrushed Mind.

From Wix to hand-coded in three working days.

The brief.

Chriselle was renting a Wix site: slow on mobile, expensive every year, and indistinguishable from every other template-built coaching site. She needed out — but not into another template. She wanted a site that felt like her brand: calm, warm, unhurried. And she needed it before her next workshop.

Three days.

Day 1 — the knowledge transfer.

I read everything she had; we talked; I wrote the copy. Three moods sketched for how “unrushed” could look on screen — she chose in ten minutes.

Day 2 — the build.

Seven pages of hand-coded HTML. Self-hosted fonts, a palette drawn from her own brand. No page-builder, no template, no bloat.

Day 3 — migration and launch.

Domain moved to Cloudflare. Google Workspace email preserved — MX, SPF and DMARC replicated record-for-record. Every old Wix URL 301-mapped. Zero downtime; her inbox never noticed.
